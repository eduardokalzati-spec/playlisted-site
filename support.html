Using Playlisted

How do I find a song?

In the video app, tap Share, then choose Playlisted. If you don't see it, tap More at the end of the row and turn Playlisted on. You can also copy the link and paste it into Playlisted.

It said it couldn't find the song.

Some songs aren't in our recognition catalog, especially unreleased tracks, fan edits and very short clips. If you know any words or the artist, search for it by name in Playlisted: tap Search by song name on the Home screen. Searching is free and doesn't use one of your free songs.

I already know the song's name.

Open Playlisted and tap Search by song name on the Home screen. Pick the song, then add it to your Apple Music playlist or open it in your music app. It's free and doesn't count toward your free songs.

It says TikTok only shows this video to signed-in users.

Some TikTok videos can only be watched while signed in, so we can't open them from a link. Screen-record a few seconds of the video with the sound on, then share the recording to Playlisted from Photos.

It found the wrong version.

Live recordings, covers and edits can match a different version of the same song. When we aren't sure, we show a few options so you can pick the right one before anything is added.

It says the post is a photo post.

Posts made of photos can't be read from a link. Screen-record the post for a few seconds with the sound on, then share the recording to Playlisted.

How do I put Playlisted first when I share?

In TikTok or Instagram, tap Share, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More. Tap Edit, tap the green + next to Playlisted, then drag it to the top. From then on Playlisted is first in the row, and it works from every app, including Photos for screen recordings. Playlisted also shows you these steps after your first song, and anytime in Settings > Faster sharing.

Can it add songs to my playlist automatically?

Yes, if you want it to. In Playlisted, open Settings > Auto-add to and pick a playlist. When we're sure of the song, it's added to that playlist by itself. When we aren't sure, we show you the options and add nothing until you pick. It's off until you turn it on, and it works with Apple Music.

Does it work with YouTube?

Not with YouTube links yet. Screen-record the Short for a few seconds with the sound on, then share the recording to Playlisted.

I use Spotify or another music app.

Everyone gets the song name and artist. Playlisted opens Spotify with the song already searched for you. For other apps, copy the name and paste it into the app's search. Adding songs to a playlist works with Apple Music only.

Free songs and Playlisted Plus

How many songs are free?

3 songs found per calendar month. Adding songs to Apple Music is always free, including songs already in your history.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Playlisted. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed on a new phone and Plus isn't showing.

Open Playlisted, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Your data

Do you keep my videos?

No. The video and audio are deleted as soon as the song is found. See the privacy policy for exactly what we keep.

How do I delete my data?

In Playlisted, open Settings and tap Delete my data. Or email playlisted.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
